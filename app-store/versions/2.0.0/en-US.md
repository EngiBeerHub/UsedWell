### Promotional Text

### Description

“Is it time to replace this Mac?”

UsedWell helps you look back on the things you use and decide for yourself whether to keep using them or move on.

Add the purchase date, price, and your own usage goal to see how long you've had an item, how far you've progressed toward your goal, and what it costs per day.

You can also keep notes on what you've actually noticed while using it.

“Maybe I've used it long enough.”
“The battery is getting worse, but everything else still works.”
“Maybe I can keep it for another year.”

UsedWell brings those pieces together so you can make the decision with your own context in front of you.

Your time with it. Your goal for it.

Set your own usage goal and see where you are today.

UsedWell shows three simple states: Still want to use it, Start considering replacement, and Goal reached.

At 90%, it's simply a point to start reflecting.

100% is not a replacement deadline.
It means you've reached the usage goal you set for yourself.

If you're still happy with the item, you can keep using it well beyond your goal.

The frustrations. The reasons to keep it.

Add dated Usage Notes whenever something stands out.

For example:

“Battery life has been getting worse.”
“Still runs smoothly.”
“Clearing some storage should keep it useful.”
“Skipped this year's upgrade.”

When you're considering a replacement, you can look back on more than numbers—you can also review how your experience with the item has changed.

There's no need to log something every day.
Add a note only when there's something worth remembering.

Cost per day. One more perspective.

UsedWell calculates your current cost per day from the purchase price and time used.

You can also compare what the cost per day would look like at your usage goal and after one more year of use.

The point isn't to “get your money's worth.”

Cost per day is simply one more perspective when you're deciding whether to replace something or keep using it.

Add a photo to make each item easier to recognize.

You can add one optional photo to each item.

Photos appear on Home, item details, and History.

If you don't add a photo, UsedWell shows a category icon instead.

Photos stay on your device.

Choose between Warm and Forest.

UsedWell includes two visual themes.

Warm uses a soft Sand / Ivory palette.
Forest uses a calm Cream / Deep Forest palette.

Light and Dark appearance continue to follow your iPhone settings.

Keep the record after you're done.

When you actually stop using an item, mark it as replaced.

The record stays in History so you can look back on your final time used, Usage Notes, final cost per day, and the period from purchase to completion.

### Review Notes

UsedWell is a local-only iPhone app that helps users review how long they have used their belongings and decide for themselves whether to keep using them or replace them.

Version 2.0 redesigns the Home experience and adds optional item photos and two selectable visual themes, Warm and Forest.

Users can record an item's name, category, purchase date, purchase price, target usage period, optional photo, and dated Usage Notes.

The app shows usage duration, progress toward the user's own target, remaining or exceeded time, current cost per day, cost per day at the target, and cost per day after another year of use.

Progress states are informational only:

- 0–89%: still using
- 90–99%: a point to start considering replacement
- 100% or more: the user's target has been reached

Reaching 100% does not automatically mark an item as replaced and does not require the user to replace it. Users can continue using an item beyond their target.

When the user actually stops using an item, they can explicitly mark it as replaced. The item remains available in History with its final usage duration, Usage Notes, and cost per day.

Item photos are optional, selected from the Photos picker, and stored locally on the device. No photo is uploaded to a server.

Warm / Forest is a local visual preference only. Light / Dark appearance continues to follow the iPhone's system setting.

Currency values are not converted when the device Region changes. The app formats stored amounts using the current Region and shows a notice when a Region change may affect the interpretation of previously entered prices.

No account, sign-in, cloud synchronization, paid content, subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertisements, external hardware, or automatic replacement recommendation is required.

All user data is stored locally on the device. Notification permission is optional and is used only for locally scheduled usage-progress reminders.
