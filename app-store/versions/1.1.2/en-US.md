### Promotional Text

Used it long enough? Review how long you've owned it, your goal, notes, and cost per day to decide whether to keep it or replace it.

### Description

“Is it time to replace this Mac?”

UsedWell helps you look back on how long you've owned and used the things you care about, so you can decide when it feels right to replace them.

Add the purchase date and price to see how long you've owned an item, your progress toward your goal, and its cost per day.

“Maybe I've used it long enough.”
“Maybe it's still good for another year.”

UsedWell gives you the information to decide for yourself.

How long do you want to use it?

Set your own usage goal and see how far you've come.

UsedWell can notify you when you reach 90% and 100% of your goal.

100% is not a replacement deadline.
If you're still happy with the item, you can keep using it well beyond your goal.

Keep notes on what you notice.

“Battery life has been getting worse lately.”
“Still happy with it.”
“Storage is starting to feel tight.”

Add dated Usage Notes whenever something stands out while you're using an item.

When you're considering a replacement, you can look back on not only the numbers, but also how your experience with the item has changed over time.

What does it cost per day?

See your current cost per day, what it would be at your usage goal, and what it could look like if you kept the item for another year.

Cost per day is just one more piece of information to help you decide whether to replace something or keep using it.

Keep a record after you're done with it.

When you finish using an item, mark it as replaced and keep it in your history.

You can look back later on how long you used it and its final cost per day.

Key features

• Add, edit, and delete items
• Track time owned and your usage goal
• See progress toward your goal
• Add dated Usage Notes
• See current cost per day
• Compare cost per day at your goal and after another year
• Optional notifications at 90% and 100% of your goal
• Mark items as replaced and keep a usage history
• Light and dark appearance

Don't replace it on a whim.

UsedWell helps you review time owned, cost, and your own notes so you can decide whether to replace something or keep using it.

Your data stays on your device.
No account is required.

### Review Notes

UsedWell is a local-only personal item usage tracking app that helps users review how long they have owned and used their belongings and consider when to replace them.

Version 1.1.2 adds English localization and region-aware presentation for dates and currency. It does not add account-based, cloud, paid, advertising, or automatic replacement recommendation functionality.

Users can record an item's purchase date, purchase price, and target usage period, then review the current usage duration, progress toward the target, cost per day, and dated Usage Notes.

Currency values are not converted when the device Region changes. The app displays amounts using the current Region's currency format and shows a one-time notice when a Region change may affect the meaning of previously entered prices.

No account, sign-in, paid content, subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertisements, or external hardware are required.

All user data is stored locally on the device. Notification permission is optional and is used only for usage-progress reminders scheduled on the device.
